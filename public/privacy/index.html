Privacy

Capacity Map stores your team, service, resource, and job-plan records only in this browser’s IndexedDB. It does not send those records to us, use analytics, or track employees.

A license token is stored in this browser only to enable Capacity Map Plus. When online, it may be checked with Sociobot’s licensing service no more than once per day. CSV exports and imports happen only when you choose them.
